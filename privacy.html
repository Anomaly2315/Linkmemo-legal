LinkMemo Privacy Policy

LinkMemo is a local-first link manager. This policy describes the data used by local features, LinkMemo accounts, Google Play purchases, and optional AI enhancement.

Document version
1.2
Effective date
17 August 2026
Developer
LinkMemo, Hong Kong

1. Local-first data

Links, titles, webpage summaries, notes, folders, pinned and private status, attachments, recordings, cached webpage images, and preferences are stored on the device. A LinkMemo account does not synchronize the local links database, folders, attachments, recordings, Private Space database, or backups.

2. Device features and local protection

The local database and note attachments use a device-generated key held in system secure storage. Private Space uses Android system authentication; LinkMemo receives only success, cancellation, or failure and does not read fingerprints, facial data, screen-lock passwords, or patterns. Clipboard detection runs only while enabled and the app is in use. Share import, photo, camera, microphone, and file access occur only for features initiated by the user.

3. LinkMemo account and server data

Local core features work without signing in. Google sign-in and Supabase process the identity tokens needed for authentication. The backend stores the Supabase UID and necessary account details supplied by Google, such as email address and display name. The account is used for purchase ownership, restoring Full and LinkMemo Pro entitlements across devices, AI quota, and account deletion. The Google Play paying account may differ from the Google account used for LinkMemo.

4. Web analysis and Gemini AI

Normal metadata analysis connects to the target website and public endpoints. When the user deliberately runs LinkMemo Pro AI enhancement, the request sent through a Supabase Edge Function to Google Gemini may contain the title, description, source site and domain, full or canonical URL, a webpage excerpt limited to about 4,000 characters, image-candidate URLs and descriptions, and, when useful and safely available, one representative image. Gemini is not authorized to sign in to websites, this feature does not use Google Search, and it does not watch videos.

Private Space data is subject to an explicit in-app disclosure before it is sent; the user may choose not to see the reminder again. Original attachments, recordings, purchase credentials, login credentials, the complete local database, and unrelated personal data are not sent to AI. AI output is stored in the link’s local record.

5. Google Play purchases and server entitlements

Google Play handles Full and LinkMemo Pro payment, subscription, refund, and revocation. Purchase credentials are verified by the LinkMemo backend with the Google Play Developer API and bound to a Supabase UID. An order ID or purchase token is not account identity. One purchase credential can be bound to only one LinkMemo account and is not transferred automatically. Server entitlements are authoritative; the app stores only a recent trusted offline cache. The backend also stores purchase state, subscription periods, AI quota, idempotent request state, and necessary security logs.

6. Backups and local retention

A full backup may contain regular and private links, folders, valid attachments, and app settings. It excludes Recently Deleted. Every user may export a backup; importing one requires Full. Import merges without deleting existing data or replacing device settings. Backups have no separate password protection and must be kept securely. Local data remains until it is deleted, app data is cleared, or LinkMemo is uninstalled. Exported or shared copies are controlled by the user.

7. Delete a LinkMemo account

Deletion can be requested inside LinkMemo or by emailing zengqb2315509134@gmail.com. For an email request, use the email address associated with the LinkMemo account and complete reasonable ownership verification. Never send a password, login token, purchase token, or payment credential.

If no LinkMemo Pro period is active, the server account and associated account data are deleted as soon as reasonably possible. If a free or paid period is already active, LinkMemo stops future auto-renewal and schedules deletion after the current period ends. After deletion, Full, LinkMemo Pro, Gift history, and AI quota cannot be restored or transferred. Device-local links, attachments, recordings, and backups are not deleted remotely.

The raw purchase token is deleted. A non-reassignable token hash and minimum status record may be retained without an active account association to prevent an old purchase from being claimed by another account and where required for legal, financial, security, or fraud-prevention purposes.

8. Service providers, security, and contact

LinkMemo does not sell user data. Google provides sign-in and Google Play, Supabase provides identity, database, and Edge Functions, and Google Gemini processes AI requests. These providers may process data outside the user’s region under their applicable terms. LinkMemo does not place full Authorization values, purchase tokens, Gemini keys, or complete AI request bodies in app logs. Reduced or hashed event identifiers, error classes, usage, and security information may be retained for service operation, duplicate-charge prevention, diagnostics, and abuse prevention.

完整备份可能包含普通和私密链接、文件夹、有效附件及应用设置，不包含最近删除。所有用户均可导出备份，导入需要Full。导入采用合并方式，不删除现有数据，也不覆盖设备设置。备份没有独立密码保护，应妥善保管。本地数据会保留到用户删除、清除应用数据或卸载；已经导出或分享的副本由用户管理。

用户可以在LinkMemo应用内申请删除，也可以发送邮件至zengqb2315509134@gmail.com。邮件申请应使用LinkMemo账号关联邮箱并完成合理的归属确认。请勿发送密码、登录Token、purchaseToken或付款凭证。

6. バックアップとローカル保持

完全バックアップには通常・非公開リンク、フォルダ、有効な添付、設定が含まれる場合があります。書き出しは全ユーザーが利用でき、読込みにはFullが必要で、既存データへマージします。独立したパスワードはありません。ローカルデータは削除、アプリデータ消去、アンインストールまで保持され、書き出したコピーはユーザーが管理します。
